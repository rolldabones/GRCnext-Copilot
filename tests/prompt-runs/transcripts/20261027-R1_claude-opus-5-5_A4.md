## 4. Provisions that obstruct exit

- **4.3 Unilateral immediate suspension.** No notice, no cure, no continuity and no trigger for assistance or data return. The standard of "reasonably believes" is low and unverifiable in real time.
- **4.1 Notice period and fee.** The 180-day notice is twice the 90-day non-renewal notice. This makes non-renewal the cheaper path, but only if the window is tracked.
- **2.2 Sole remedy.** This channels service failure into credits and may prevent sustained underperformance from reaching material breach.
- **3.1 Uncontrolled subcontracting.** Fourth parties, their jurisdictions and any concentration they create are invisible. The obligation to "remain responsible" does not give Bank B any exit or audit lever.
- **6.1 and 6.2 together.** Data returned up to day 30 can be deleted from day 30 onward. That leaves Bank B a window of 30 days or less to validate usability, with no acceptance gate before deletion.
- **7.1 and 7.2 Liability.** Losses from a failed transition (customer harm, regulatory consequences) may be treated as indirect or consequential, or may exceed the cap of 12 months' fees.
