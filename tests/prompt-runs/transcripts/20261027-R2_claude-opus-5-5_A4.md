## Rating: RED

The three principal reasons:

1. **The Provider holds an immediate switch and the Customer holds none.** Clause 4.3 lets Vendor A suspend immediately on its own reasonable belief. The Customer has no matching suspension, step-in or immediate exit right. Termination assistance (5.1) and data return (6.1) start only on termination, not on suspension. If Vendor A suspends lawfully, Bank B may lack a for-cause termination route. It would then face a 180-day convenience notice plus a six-month fee while the service is down. That reading needs counsel's confirmation.
2. **The data exit path is late, format-constrained and races deletion.** Data comes back up to 30 days after termination, in the Provider's "standard export format," with no usability standard and no pre-termination access. Deletion follows within 60 days of termination. Bank B gets at most a 30-day window to validate the export before deletion may begin, and no clause preserves records needed for any retention obligation.
3. **Fourth-party exposure is uncontrolled and unremedied.** Clause 3.1 permits subcontracting of any part of the service without consent or notice. There are no audit, incident notification, location or change-of-control provisions. Clause 2.2 makes capped service credits the sole remedy for service-level failure. For a regulated bank's real-time payments service, this leaves fourth-party and jurisdictional concentration invisible and leaves Bank B with little leverage.

⧉ **Single largest gap:** Nothing in this contract gives Bank B a way to keep Service B within any plausible real-time payments tolerance if Vendor A stops performing. Every contractual exit runs in weeks to months. Meeting tolerance would therefore depend entirely on a pre-positioned alternate gateway or manual switch. That arrangement is Not evidenced.

- **7.1 and 7.2 Liability limits.** The cap and the consequential-loss exclusion limit recovery of exit and disruption costs.
- **No survival clause.** It is unclear whether the 5 and 6 obligations survive termination for cause.
