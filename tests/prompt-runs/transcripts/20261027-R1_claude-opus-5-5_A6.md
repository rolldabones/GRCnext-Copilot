## Why the email cannot support Demonstrated

1. **It is a representation, not evidence of execution.** It is the weakest evidence type in the hierarchy. It is not a log, ticket, exercise record or observed test.
2. **The exercise tier is unknown.** "Tested" could mean anything from a document walkthrough (T0) to a full end-to-end rehearsal (T3). Tier caps maturity. Without the tier, Exits and Exercises cannot reach L3.
3. **No tolerance and no clock.** Demonstrated requires a tolerance result of Met, with both the onset event and the stabilization event stated. The email gives neither and does not show that a tolerance existed in 2021.
4. **The world has moved since 2021.** Any of these may have changed: the contract (renewals, termination and transition-assistance terms), data volume and formats, architecture and integrations, substitute provider capacity, access credentials and tooling, fourth-party dependencies, jurisdictional constraints and the people who held the knowledge.
5. **"All good" is not a pass criterion.** A test that was not measured against defined pass and fail criteria cannot show that the tolerance was met.
