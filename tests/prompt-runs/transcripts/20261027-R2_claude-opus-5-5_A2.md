## What Level 4 Requires

L4 Instrumented is scored per domain and per critical service. It requires all of the following:

1. **Monitoring.** The capability is monitored continuously, not just documented.
2. **Periodic exercise.** At least a T3 end-to-end rehearsal of the governing scenario. Tabletops (T0 or T1) do not reach L3, let alone L4.
3. **Current metrics.** Metrics with defined freshness and triggers tied to the impact tolerance.
4. **Verified evidence.** Execution records, logs, exercise records or production events, not self-attestation.
5. **Closed loops.** Corrective actions closed, with closure verified and remediation retested.

Dashboards alone do not earn L4. The structural rating (Constraint Level) is the minimum of Pipes, Switches and Exits. If any one of those is weaker, the service is not L4.
