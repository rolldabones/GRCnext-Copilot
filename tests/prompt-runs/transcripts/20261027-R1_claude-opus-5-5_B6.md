- **(a)** Vendor A alleges that *you* breached. Your termination options may then be affected by the dispute, and Vendor A may hold its own termination or suspension rights.

## 2. Operational observation (for counsel to test)

- **Non-renewal** under the renewal window, if the contract permits it. This route may not depend on proving breach.
- **Termination for cause**, which typically depends on the breach clause, materiality, cure periods and notice formalities.
- **Termination for convenience**, if the contract provides it.

Whether any of these routes exists, and which one is safer given the pending notice, is a legal question for counsel. The renewal deadline is the hard operational constraint either way.

⧉ **Largest risk:** losing the renewal-window exit through a missed or defective notice while attention is on the breach dispute. Treat that deadline as the governing clock until counsel advises otherwise.
